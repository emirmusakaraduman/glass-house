0:50–1:10. [Screen: listing finder.] Maria starts in the listing finder. Her income and savings become a maximum price, calculated in her browser. The finder is built to ask Firecrawl for live listings below that price, sending only the property type, city and price range, never her income. The two listing sites we checked forbid scraping, so this demo shows hand-made sample listings, labelled as such. When she picks a home, the listing URL stays off-chain. The program only ever sees a salted hash and a value band.
