Step 2 · The loan

Five steps, each one a signed transaction.

1

Apply

Buyer and seller both sign; the down payment and collateral wait in escrow.

2

Approve

The underwriter checks income and value off-chain, then signs.

3

Fund

Anyone can trigger it; the program checks LTV, buffer and brakes.

4

Title confirmed

The title agent signs; escrow pays the seller.

5

Active

Anyone can flag a missed payment; the program reads the clock.

1:10–1:35. [Screen: apply, then loan page.] Maria applies, and the seller signs too, from their own phone with a co-sign link. Her down payment, a 1% fee and three installments of collateral wait in escrow. The underwriter checks her income and the value off-chain and approves. Funding is permissionless: anyone can trigger it, because the program itself checks the loan-to-value cap, the liquidity buffer and the brakes. Once the title agent confirms the transfer, escrow pays the seller and the loan is live. From then on, anyone can flag a missed payment; the program reads the clock, so no servicer can hide one.
