GlassHouse

A lending pool with glass walls.

Every loan is public

Each loan is its own Solana account: terms, payments and status. Borrowers stay pseudonymous; no names or addresses go on-chain.

Rules live in code

Minimum down payment, loan-to-value cap, liquidity buffer and lending brakes are checked by the program, not a back office.

We lose first

The platform’s own first-loss vault, then a reserve fund, take losses before a single investor does.

0:30–0:50. GlassHouse is a lending pool with glass walls. First, every loan is public: its own Solana account with terms, payments and status, while the borrower stays pseudonymous. Second, the rules live in code: the minimum down payment, the loan-to-value cap, the liquidity buffer and the lending brakes are enforced by the program.
