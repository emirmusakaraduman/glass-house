Who pays first

Investors are the last line, not the first.

  1. The borrower’s down payment and collateral
  2. The sale of the home or car
  3. Our first-loss vault, platform capital
  4. The reserve fund, fed by fees and interest
  5. Only then, investors
1:45–2:05. [Screen: transparency page.] When a loan goes bad, the loss walks down a fixed order: the borrower’s down payment and collateral, then the sale of the home, then our own first-loss vault, then the reserve fund. Investors come last. And share value is computed from the pool’s own counters, not token balances, so nobody can move the price by sending tokens to the vault.
